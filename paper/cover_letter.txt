Please consider this short communication for Knowledge-Based Systems:

Knowledge-Guided Decision Intelligence for Strategic-Value-Aware Customer Churn Intervention Under Budget Constraints

The letter treats strategic customer-value coverage and economic utility as non-identical objectives under a shared intervention budget. A leakage-safe calibrated ensemble supplies risk; customer lifetime value supplies strategic-value knowledge; a minority human-review reserve is compared with a matched CLTV-reserved offer-only policy; and an exact epsilon-constraint programme quantifies the efficiency cost of the operational 85/15 heuristic. The primary result is a 12.6 percentage-point gain in high-value churn reach versus cost-aware targeting. Dollar superiority is not claimed.

The manuscript contains two tables and two figures. Code is at https://github.com/drsaikrishnathota1/kbs-churn-decision-intelligence.
